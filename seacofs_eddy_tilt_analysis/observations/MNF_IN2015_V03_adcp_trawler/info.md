# Roughan et al. (2017): Murphy and Freddy

Roughan et al., *A tale of two eddies: The biophysical characteristics of two contrasting cyclonic eddies in the East Australian Current System*, *Journal of Geophysical Research: Oceans* (2017), [doi:10.1002/2016JC012241](https://doi.org/10.1002/2016JC012241).

The paper studied two cyclonic eddies off eastern Australia during the June 2015 IN2015_V03 voyage. Shipboard velocity sections and other observations sampled Murphy and Freddy on multiple crossings; Freddy was revisited as it moved south. See the paper's section 3.2 and Figure 3 for the MOCA centre method, Table 4 for crossing midtimes and centre estimates, and section 5.2 and Figure 10 for vertical centre trajectories.

MOCA estimates a centre from the transect-normal velocity zero crossing under locally uniform rotation. The paper reports approximate inverse-variance-weighted vertical centre-displacement rates of 3.3 km per 100 m for Murphy and 1.5 km per 100 m for Freddy. Those published MOCA quantities provide scientific context; ESP SOLO/LATTE centres and Gaussian radii are different estimands and should not be treated as reproductions of the MOCA fits.
