Finally, on IN2022_V06 Amandine and I got two looks at a compact anti-cyclone off Brisbane. First period was around 14th - 16th July, 2022. The 2nd period was around the 24th July. Unfortunately, the weather was crap, but was got some decent Triaxus tows through the eddy. 

I'd use the Ocean Current website (OceanCurrent IMOS-OceanCurrent) to eye-ball the ship track with the eddy location (use the 6 day SST and GSLA). Then have a look at the ADCP data. It might need a bit of mucking around, as we often were not doing transects across the eddy, but doing other stuff (like heading out to do mooring work, or something like that).
